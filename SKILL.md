# Meeting Summarizer

Convert raw meeting content into clean, actionable summaries.

## Core Workflow

1. Extract key topics discussed.
2. List decisions made.
3. Capture action items with owners and due dates (if mentioned).
4. Note open questions or follow-ups.
5. Keep the summary concise (under 1 page equivalent).
6. Preserve important context without unnecessary detail.

## Output Structure

**Summary**  
Brief overview.

**Decisions**  
- ...

**Action Items**  
- [Owner]: Task (due date)

**Open Questions**  
- ...
